A vehicle access system allows an owner's smartphone to unlock the doors over Bluetooth Low Energy. The phone stores a digital access credential and sends an unlock request to a body control ECU through a Bluetooth gateway. The body control ECU controls the door lock actuators. The phone can request unlocking while the vehicle is parked. A backend provisions and revokes phone credentials, but backend connectivity is not always available to the vehicle. The design states that the phone and gateway use an authenticated encrypted connection. The handling of replayed requests, lost phones, offline credential revocation and gateway-to-ECU authorization is not specified. The gateway also exposes a wired service interface for workshop maintenance; the authorization policy for that interface is unknown. Assess credential confidentiality, unlock-command authenticity and door-lock service availability. Do not assume that unspecified controls are absent.
